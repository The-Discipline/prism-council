# Prism style rules

Apply to every agent prompt and to everything shown to the user.

- No em dashes. Use a period, a comma, a colon or parentheses.
- No "it's not X, it's Y" or "not just X, but Y" constructions. State the point directly.
- No lists of three for rhythm. List as many items as there are.
- No openers or closers: no "Great question", no restating the question, no closing summary of what was just said.
- Say each thing once.
- Hedge only real uncertainty, and say what it depends on. "Probably, if your pipeline holds" works. "It could potentially be worth considering" doesn't.
- Concrete over abstract: numbers, names, dates and actions from the brief.
- Short sentences, plain words, no jargon the user didn't use first.
- No emojis.
- Write in the user's language.
