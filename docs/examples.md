# Example questions

## Pricing
- "I charge €650 a day. Should I go to €750 for new clients only, or for everyone in January?"
- "Should I switch my audits from a day rate to a fixed price of €2,500?"
- "A client wants a 20% discount for a 6-month contract. Accept, counter, or refuse?"

## Offers
- "Should my flagship offer be a 2-day workshop or a 3-month coaching program?"
- "I have three offers and sell mostly one. Should I drop the other two?"
- "Should I launch a €49 template pack or keep everything free as lead magnets?"

## Positioning
- "Should I specialize in agencies or stay generalist for freelancers and SMEs?"
- "Should I position myself as a consultant or as a trainer on my website?"

## Clients and acquisition
- "One client is 60% of my revenue and wants more days. Take them, or keep room for others?"
- "I have 5 hours a week for marketing. LinkedIn content or direct outreach?"
- "Should I keep my Malt profile active or put that time into my own website?"

## Capacity
- "Should I outsource my bookkeeping or keep doing it myself to save money?"
- "Should I take a 4-month full-time mission that pauses all my other clients?"

## Career direction
- "Should I keep freelancing or take a full-time job offer at a similar income?"

## Not for Prism (and what happens instead)
- "What am I missing in my business?" → Prism asks you to name 2 or 3 options, then decides between them
- "Write my LinkedIn post" → normal writing help
- "Review my landing page copy" → normal review
- "What's the average day rate in France?" → a factual answer
- "Should I use Notion or Trello for my to-do list?" → too cheap and reversible, answered directly
